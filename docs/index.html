Key findings

The citation network reveals a scientific landscape that is becoming more international, more Asian-centered, and marked by durable institution-to-institution partnerships.

Q1 — Integration over time

Participating countries grew from around 65 to over 110 between 2005 and 2025, while over 80% of citation links crossed national borders throughout the period. The academic network is integrating, not fragmenting.

Q2 — Shifting hubs

Chinese institutions shifted from net importers to major exporters across multiple fields. French institutions such as CNRS, INRIA, and CEA consolidated as dominant domestic producers, while several older European and Japanese hubs lost relative ground.

Q3 — Strongest reciprocal bonds

Caltech and the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics form the most enduring bond, appearing in all 21 years with a peak weight of 285 in 2021. CNRS–CEA remained strong in Magnetic Thin Films, while Complex Network Analysis shifted toward Chinese domestic clusters by 2018.
